# The Explain using AI button

The Explain using AI button writes a plain answer about a number, a term, or a question on the page. The answer opens in a panel on the right side of the screen. The panel writes in plain words, using the company or sector you are looking at.

There are two ways to start. The first way is to highlight text. Select any words or any number in the main part of the page. A small Explain using AI button appears just above what you selected. Click the button. The second way is the Explain using AI button next to each question in the Qualitative section. Click that button to get a written answer to that exact question.

The panel shows a short loading line while it works. The finished answer is text you can read. Under some answers a Vault sources line lists the books the answer drew from. For a question answer, a Copy to my answer button puts the text into your answer box. A Regenerate button writes a fresh answer.

## What the button does not do

The button explains ideas. The button does not tell you to buy or sell anything. No answer is a recommendation to trade. Read the answer to understand a term or a number, then decide for yourself.

Close the panel with the X in its top corner, or press the Escape key.
