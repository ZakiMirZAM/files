# The Notebook

The Notebook is a place to write short notes while you work. You can jot down a thought, a question, or something you want to check later. Your notes save to your account on their own. You do not press a save button.

To open the Notebook, click the notebook button in the top bar. The button sits near the right side of the bar. The Notebook slides in as a panel. Click the same button again to close the panel.

The Notebook holds a list of notes. Click New note to start a fresh one. Click any note in the list to open it and write. Each note has a title you can change. To rename or delete a note, hover over it in the list and use the small pencil or trash buttons that appear. Delete asks you to click twice, so you do not remove a note by accident.

## Where your notes appear

Your notes belong to your account, not to one page. The same notes are there on every page of the app. You can move from the Economic page to a company page and your notes stay with you.

The Notebook stays separate from your written answers in the Qualitative section. Use the Notebook for loose notes. Use the answer boxes for the answers you want in your final report.
