# The Notebook

The Notebook is a place to write short notes while you work: a thought, a question, or something you want to check later. Notes save to your account on their own, so there is no save button to press.

To open it, click the notebook button in the top bar, near the right side. The Notebook slides in as a panel, and clicking the same button again closes it.

Inside is a list of notes. Click New note to start a fresh one, or click any note in the list to open it and write. Each note has a title you can change. To rename or delete a note, hover over it in the list and use the small pencil or trash buttons that appear. Deleting asks you to click twice, so you cannot remove a note by accident.

## Where your notes appear

Your notes belong to your account, not to one page. The same notes are there on every page, so you can move from the Economic page to a company page and your notes come with you.

The Notebook is separate from your written answers in the Qualitative section. Use the Notebook for loose notes as you go. Use the answer boxes for the answers you want to appear in your final report.
