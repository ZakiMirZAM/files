# Education, not advice

This app is a learning tool. The purpose is to teach you how company analysis works and how a value estimate is built. The app does not tell you to buy or sell anything.

Nothing here is financial advice. The numbers and explanations are there to help you understand, not to direct your money. Any decision about your own money is yours alone, and you may want to speak with a qualified professional before acting.

Always check primary sources before you act on anything you see. A primary source is the original document, such as a company's official financial filing or a figure straight from the data provider. The app pulls in data and explains it, but data can be out of date or wrong. Confirming the original keeps you from acting on a stale or mistaken number.

In short: educational tool. Not financial advice. Always verify primary sources.
