# Education, not advice

This app is a learning tool. Its purpose is to teach you how company analysis works and how a value estimate is built. It does not tell you to buy or sell anything.

Nothing here is financial advice. The numbers and explanations are there to help you understand, not to direct your money. Any decision about your own money is yours alone, and you may want to speak with a qualified professional before acting.

Check primary sources before you act on anything you see here. A primary source is the original document, such as a company's official financial filing, or a figure taken straight from the data provider. The app pulls in data and explains it, but data can be out of date or simply wrong, and confirming the original is what keeps you from acting on a stale or mistaken number.

In short: educational tool. Not financial advice. Always verify primary sources.
