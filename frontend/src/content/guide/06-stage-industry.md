# Stage 3: the industry

An industry is a smaller group inside a sector. Semiconductors sit inside the technology sector. Insurance sits inside the financials sector. The Industry page is the third step. The page narrows the focus from a broad sector to one tight group of competitors.

The question this stage answers has two parts. Which smaller group looks strong, and which companies sit inside it.

## What you see and click

The page opens with a list of industries. The industries are grouped under their parent sector to keep the list short. Click a sector name to expand its industries. The "Expand all" control opens every group at once. A search box at the top filters the whole list by name.

Each industry is a card. Click a card to open its detail page. The detail page shows an Industry Scorecard. The scorecard reports the same kind of measures used for sectors, now for this one group. The measures cover growth, profit margins, and debt.

To see the companies in the industry, click the button "Compare the companies in this industry". The button opens a comparison page. The comparison page lists and ranks the individual companies. Each company is a row you can click to open its own page, which is Stage 4.
