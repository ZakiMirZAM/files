# Stage 3: the industry

An industry is a smaller group inside a sector. Semiconductors sit inside the technology sector. Insurance sits inside the financials sector. This stage narrows the focus from a broad sector to one tight group of competitors, which is the level where companies actually fight each other for customers.

The question this stage answers has two parts. Which of these narrower groups looks strong, and which companies sit inside the one you choose.

## What you see and click

The page opens with a list of industries grouped under their parent sector, which keeps the list short. Click a sector name to expand its industries, or use "Expand all" to open every group at once. The search box at the top filters the whole list by name.

Each industry is a card. Click a card to open its detail page, which shows an Industry Scorecard. The scorecard reports the same kinds of measure used for sectors, now for this one group: growth, profit margins, and debt.

To see the companies inside the industry, click "Compare the companies in this industry". That opens the comparison page, which is Stage 4. From there, clicking a company symbol opens its own full report, which is Stage 5.
