# Stage 6: the Final Report

The Final Report is the last stop. Open it from the Final Report item in the left menu once you have worked through the earlier stages. It holds a short set of review questions that turn the analysis back on you and ask how confident and how prepared you are before you act. The point is to catch the case where the numbers look fine but you cannot actually explain why you believe them.

Each question has a text box. Type your answer and it saves to your account on its own a moment after you stop typing, confirmed by a small "saved" note. There is no save button to press. Your written answers from the earlier stages are kept the same way, attached to the company or industry you were working on, so they appear here without you copying anything across.

## Keeping a copy

The app has a separate Notebook for free writing. Open it with the pen icon in the top bar. Use the Notebook to gather loose thinking in one place, and to save a note as a PDF file you can keep or print. Use the review questions here for the final check before you decide anything.
