# Stage 6: the Final Report

The Final Report is the last stop. Open the page from the Final Report item in the left menu, after you work through the earlier stages. The page holds a short set of review questions. The questions turn the analysis on yourself and ask how confident and how prepared you are before you act.

Each question has a text box. Type your answer in the box. The answer saves to your account on its own, a moment after you stop typing. A small "saved" note confirms the save. There is no save button to press. Your written answers from the earlier stages are kept the same way, attached to each company or industry you worked on.

## Keeping a copy

The app has a separate Notebook for free writing. Open the Notebook with the pen icon in the top bar. The Notebook lets you write notes and gather your thinking in one place. The Notebook can save a note as a PDF file, which is a document you can keep or print. Use the Notebook when you want one document to take away. The review questions on this page are for the final check before you decide.
