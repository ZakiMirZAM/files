# A short glossary

These are the terms you will meet most often across the app, each with a one-line plain meaning. You do not need to memorize them. The Definitions panel covers these and many more in full, and it is always one click away.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Share | One small unit of ownership in a company. |
| Share price | The amount one share costs to buy or sell right now. |
| Valuation | An estimate of what a company is worth. |
| Intrinsic value | What a company is worth based on the cash the business earns, not its share price. |
| Margin of safety | The gap between your value estimate and the price, which is your room to be wrong. |
| Sector | A broad group of companies in similar lines of work, such as energy or health care. |
| Industry | A narrower group inside a sector, such as oil drilling inside the energy sector. |
| Profit margin | The share of sales a company keeps as profit, shown as a percent. |
| Cash flow | The actual cash that moves into and out of a company. |
| Debt | Money a company has borrowed and must pay back. |
| Dividend | A cash payment a company sends to its shareholders out of profit. |
| Earnings | A company's profit over a period, what is left after costs. |
| Revenue | The total money a company takes in from sales before any costs. |
