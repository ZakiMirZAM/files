# The Definitions panel

The Definitions panel explains one measure in plain words. A measure is a single number the app reports, such as a profit margin or a debt level. The panel opens on the right side of the screen and is the fastest way to learn a term without leaving the page you are working on.

To open it, click a highlighted measure name. Those names are highlighted everywhere in the app: in tables, in charts, and in section text. One click and the panel slides in with that measure's definition.

The panel has two parts. The top is a small chart of the measure's history, so you can see how the number changed over past years and whether the latest reading is normal for this company. The bottom is the written definition, which says what the measure is, how the app works it out, and how to read a high or a low value. That last part is the one worth reading closely, because most measures are only good or bad in context.

Each definition has a number in the corner of the panel, which is just a label for that entry. You can scroll inside the panel to read the full text, and use the widen button at the top for the longer entries.

Open a different measure at any time by clicking another highlighted name, and the panel switches to it. To close the panel, click the X in its top corner, or press the Escape key.
