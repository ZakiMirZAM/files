# The Definitions panel

The Definitions panel is a side panel that explains one measure in plain words. A measure is a single number the app reports, such as a profit margin or a debt level. The panel opens on the right side of the screen.

To open the panel, click a highlighted measure name. Measure names are highlighted across the app. You will find them in tables, in charts, and in section text. Click the name once. The panel slides in and shows the definition for that measure.

The panel has two parts. The top part is a small chart of the measure's history. The chart shows how the number changed over past years, so you can see the trend at a glance. The bottom part is the written definition. The definition says what the measure is and how the app works it out. It also explains how to read a high or a low value.

Each definition has a number in the corner of the panel. The number is just a label for that entry. You can scroll inside the panel to read the full text.

Open a different measure at any time. Click another highlighted name and the panel switches to that one. To close the panel, click the X in its top corner, or press the Escape key.
