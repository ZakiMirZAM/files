# The order of the stages

The app works from the big picture down to one company. The order is fixed. There are five stages.

The first stage is the economy. The economy is the overall activity of a whole country. It covers things like growth, jobs, and interest rates. The second stage is a sector. A sector is a broad group of companies in similar work, such as technology or energy. The third stage is an industry. An industry is a narrower slice inside a sector, such as software inside technology. The fourth stage compares several companies in the same industry side by side. The fifth stage studies one company on its own.

Each stage narrows the search. Each stage also rules options in or out, so you carry fewer choices into the next one.

## What each stage decides

| Stage | What it helps you decide |
|---|---|
| Economy | Whether the overall conditions favor buying companies now or waiting |
| Sector | Which broad groups look healthy and worth a closer look |
| Industry | Which narrow group inside a sector to focus on |
| Comparison | Which companies in that group stand out against the others |
| One company | Whether a single company is worth its current price |
