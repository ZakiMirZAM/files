# The order of the stages

The app works from the big picture down to one company. There are five stages of analysis, and then a report that collects what you decided.

The first stage is the economy, meaning the overall activity of a whole country. It covers growth, jobs, and interest rates. The second stage is a sector, a broad group of companies doing similar work, such as technology or energy. The third stage is an industry, a narrower slice inside a sector, such as software inside technology. The fourth stage puts several companies from the same industry side by side. The fifth stage studies one company on its own. The Final Report then gathers your answers into one document.

Each stage narrows the field, and each one rules options in or out, so you carry fewer choices into the next. You can move through the stages in any order you like, but the fixed order is the one that gives every number its context.

## What each stage decides

| Stage | What it helps you decide |
|---|---|
| Economy | Whether overall conditions favor buying companies now or waiting |
| Sector | Which broad groups look healthy and worth a closer look |
| Industry | Which narrow group inside a sector to focus on |
| Comparison | Which companies in that group stand out against the others |
| One company | Whether a single company is worth its current price |
| Final Report | Whether you are confident and prepared enough to act |
