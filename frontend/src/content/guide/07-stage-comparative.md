# Stage 4: compare companies

The Comparative page lines up the main companies in one industry side by side. Open the page from the Comparative item in the left menu, or from the Industry page using the "Compare the companies in this industry" button. The companies then appear as a table.

The table is a grid. Each row is one company, shown by its stock symbol (the short code that identifies a company, for example AAPL for Apple). Each column is one measure, such as a profit number or a debt number. Related columns sit together under grouped headings.

## How to read the colors

The page tints each cell to rank the companies down that column. Green means the company looks better on that one measure. Red means it looks worse. Reading down a single column tells you which companies lead and which lag on that measure.

## What you click

Two tables appear. The first is a value ranking. The second is a company scorecard. Above the ranking are two filters. One toggle limits the list to United States and developed-market companies. One box sets a smallest company size, typed in billions of dollars. Click a company symbol to open its full report on the Individual page. Click any number cell to open the side panel, which draws that company's history for that measure. The question this page answers: which companies look best on the numbers.
