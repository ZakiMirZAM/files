# Start here

This app teaches you how to study a company and judge what it is worth. You do not need a finance background. You do not need to have invested before. Every term is explained in plain words the first time it appears.

You do not have to read this guide before you start. The app is built to be learned by using it. If you want to get moving right now, the short list below is the fastest way in.

## Your first ten minutes

1. Open the Economic page from the left menu. Click one row in the Metrics list. A chart opens with a plain definition under it. That is the shape of the whole app: click a thing, read what it means.
2. Click any highlighted measure name. A panel opens on the right with the definition and a small chart. That panel is available on every page.
3. Open the Individual page and type a symbol you know, such as AAPL. Click Analyze. You now have a full company report in front of you.
4. On that report, compare the current share price with the estimate of value per share. That single comparison is what the whole app is building toward.
5. When something confuses you, highlight it and click Explain using AI. You get a plain answer written for the thing you are looking at.

That is enough to be useful. Everything after this is detail you can pick up as you need it.

## Two ways to learn the rest

The in-app tour points at each part of the screen while you use it. Open the help menu in the top bar and choose the welcome tour, or choose the tour for the page you are on.

The sections below cover the same ground in writing, at your own pace. They explain each page and each tool, what you click, and what appears when you do. Read them in order, or jump straight to the page you have open.
