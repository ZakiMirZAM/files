# Start here

This app is for people who want to learn how to study a company and judge what it is worth. You do not need any finance background. You do not need to have invested before. Plain language is used throughout, and every term is explained the first time it appears.

The app helps you do two things. First, it walks you through one company step by step. Second, it helps you form a view on what that company is worth.

There are two ways to learn the app. You can take the in-app tour, which points out each part of the screen as you go. Or you can read the sections below at your own pace. Both cover the same ground.

The sections that follow explain each page and each tool. They describe what you click and what you see on the screen. Read them in order, or jump to the page you are looking at right now.
