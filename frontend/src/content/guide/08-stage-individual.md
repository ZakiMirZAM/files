# Stage 5: one company

This is the full report for one company, and it is where all the earlier stages point. Open it from the Individual item in the left menu. Type a stock symbol in the search box, which is the short code that identifies a company, for example MSFT for Microsoft. Matching companies appear as you type and you can click one. There are also quick-pick buttons for a few well-known companies. Click Analyze to load the report.

## What the page shows

At the top is the current share price, the price one share trades at right now. Next to it is an estimate of value per share, which is what the company's own numbers suggest a share could be worth. Comparing those two figures is the point of this page. When the estimate sits well above the price, the gap between them is your margin of safety, which is the room you have to be wrong and still do all right.

Below that are the company's results over several years, covering profit, cash, and debt, each with a multi-year chart. Read the direction of the line before you read any single year. Near the end is a checklist that scores the company against fixed pass marks and asks you to judge the things numbers do not capture.

## Getting plain explanations

Click any measure name to open the side panel with its definition. Each written question has an "Explain using AI" button, which draws a sourced answer from the app's financial-text library and the web, using this company's own figures. The question this page answers: is this one company worth more than its current price.
