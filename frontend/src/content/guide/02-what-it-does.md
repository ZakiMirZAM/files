# What this app does

The app answers one question: is this company worth more or less than the price it trades at today. The share price is what one share costs to buy right now. That price is easy to look up. What the company is worth is the part you have to work out, and that is what the app helps you do.

Worth here means an estimate of value. The estimate is built from the company's profits and the cash it produces over time. Profit is what the company keeps after its costs. Cash is the money that actually moves in and out of the business. A company that earns steady profits and cash for many years is worth more than one that does not. The estimate puts a number on that idea, so you can hold it up against the price.

The app does not drop you straight into one company. It walks you through stages in a set order, starting wide and narrowing down. That order is deliberate. A company that looks cheap can still be a poor idea if its whole industry is shrinking, and you only see that by looking at the industry first.

Each stage feeds the next. What you learn about the economy shapes how you read a sector. What you learn about a sector shapes how you read an industry. By the last stage you are looking at one company with a reason behind every number you read.
