# What this app does

The app estimates what one company is worth. Then it compares that estimate to the current share price. The share price is what one share of the company costs to buy right now.

Worth here means an estimate of value. The estimate is built from the company's profits and the cash it produces over time. Profit is what the company keeps after its costs. Cash is the money that actually moves in and out of the business. A company that earns steady profits and cash over many years is worth more than one that does not. The estimate puts a number on that idea.

The app does not drop you straight into one company. It guides you through stages in a set order. Each stage looks at a wider picture first, then narrows down.

Each stage also feeds the next one. What you learn about the wider economy shapes how you read a single industry. What you learn about an industry shapes how you read one company. By the last stage, you are looking at one company with context behind every number.
