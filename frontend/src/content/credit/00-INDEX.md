# Credit and Banking Metrics Library

A reference library of 17 credit-cycle and banking-system metrics, grounded in Arnold Ziegel's *Fundamentals of Credit and Credit Analysis*. They cover the macro "Condition" (the third of the five C's) that sets every borrower's terms: the price of default risk, the credit cycle, asset quality, and bank capital and liquidity. Each entry follows the identical six-part template:

1. **Definition**: what the metric measures, units, frequency, and how it is constructed
2. **Why this metric matters in finding the best sectors**: the analytical reason the metric is used
3. **What it tells you**: a table classifying all eleven GICS sectors for each reading
4. **Finding the best sectors to invest in**: the sector-by-sector mechanical earnings link
5. **Metrics to analyze alongside it**: paired metrics and how combining them sharpens the call
6. **Ten precise qualitative questions**: structural questions that probe what creates the reading
